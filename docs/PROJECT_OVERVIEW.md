# Project Overview

## Project identity

- **Project:** Digital Health Plus
- **Publication platform:** Blogger
- **Live URL:** https://digitalhealthplus.blogspot.com
- **Source-control repository:** https://github.com/abdulqawiqaid/digital-health-plus

## Mission

Make reliable, practical knowledge about digital health and healthcare innovation accessible to healthcare professionals, researchers, students, and interested readers.

## Main subject areas

1. Digital health strategy and transformation
2. Health information systems and electronic health records
3. Medical informatics and interoperability
4. Artificial intelligence and decision support in healthcare
5. Healthcare quality, patient safety, and governance
6. Health data privacy, cybersecurity, and responsible innovation

## Intended audiences

- Healthcare professionals and administrators
- Public-health practitioners
- Health informatics and IT professionals
- Researchers, educators, and students
- Readers interested in evidence-informed healthcare innovation

## Project boundaries

The blog is an educational publication, not a substitute for professional medical assessment, local clinical protocols, or official regulatory guidance. Content should not expose patient-identifiable information or confidential institutional material.

## Repository role

GitHub is used for documentation, approved technical assets, issue tracking, and change history. Blogger remains the publishing platform unless a future migration is explicitly planned and approved.
